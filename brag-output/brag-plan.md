# Brag Plan — Expenso

## Angle
A clean app-store style launch video showing Expenso as the zero-hassle personal finance tracker for India. No signups, no servers, just your browser.

## Hook
Bold "₹" symbol animates in on a dark indigo background, then the tagline: "Track every rupee." — immediate, specific, visual.

## Highlights
1. **Dashboard** — the four colorful metric cards (Total Balance, Monthly Income, Monthly Expenses, Savings Rate) appear one by one
2. **Reports** — a PDF report page fans out, showing the landscape A4 with tables and charts
3. **Zero Backend** — "No account. No server. Your browser is the vault." text punches in

## Punchline
Expenso logo + "Start tracking → expensobypartha.netlify.app" with a subtle pulse

## Tone
`app-store` — Clean feature cards, smooth slides, professional but warm

## Visual Identity
- Font: Inter (sans-serif)
- Primary: Indigo #6366f1
- Accent gradient cards: Blue (#3b82f6), Green (#10b981), Red (#ef4444), Purple (#8b5cf6)
- Background: Dark slate #0f172a with soft indigo radial glows
- Text: White on dark
